How this works

The build sheet, in plain English.

What you're looking at

A small private web app that shows what your AI assistant is doing for you: active work, things waiting on your call, and recently finished work. Each card has the current status, the last meaningful update, and the next move.

How it stays current

Your assistant's own task list is the source of truth. As work is started, updated, or completed, the assistant writes a short plain-English entry into a small database behind this app. The app only reads it. If something looks stale, pull the page or tap Refresh.

Who can see it

  • Only someone who can receive email at your address can sign in (a sign-in link is emailed, no password).
  • The database enforces this again on its side: it only hands rows to your signed-in identity (row-level security).
  • The app is not listed anywhere; only people you share the link and sign-in email with can reach it.

The pieces

  • This page: a static HTML/JS app hosted on Surge. It receives only the app files.
  • Sign-in + storage: Supabase (a hosted Postgres database with email sign-in). It receives your email address for sign-in links, and stores the project list: titles, statuses, updates, and next moves.
  • Writing updates: your assistant (or any script you run), using the private service key. The key is never in the app or the browser.
  • Source code: this repository. Fork it and point it at your own Supabase project.

What it costs

Nothing right now. Surge and Supabase both run on free plans that comfortably cover a single-user app (Supabase free: 500 MB database, 2 GB bandwidth/month; free projects pause after about a week of no activity and unpause on the next visit). No credit card is on file for any of it. If a provider ever charges, that needs your approval first.

If you want it changed or gone

Ask your assistant. The list can add sections (money, home, people), show history per project, or send a nudge when something starts waiting on you. The whole thing — app, database, repo — can also be exported or deleted on request.
